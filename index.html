About Frigonais

Nearly Three Decades of
Fruit Processing Excellence

Founded in 1996 as a family enterprise, Frigonais has grown into one of Serbia's leading fruit processing companies with over 120 employees and a global reach spanning three continents.

Our headquarters are based in Niš, while production facilities are strategically located in the Kuršumlija spa region — an ecologically pristine area renowned for its exceptional fruit-growing conditions. This unique terroir provides the foundation for our premium-quality raw materials.

Through both hot and cold processing methods, we transform locally sourced fruits into a comprehensive range of B2B ingredients: frozen fruit (IQF), jams, fruit purées, thermostable masses, yogurt fruit preparations, and custom fruit fillings — serving food manufacturers, bakeries, dairies, and distributors worldwide.

Our Process — Field to Delivery
1
Raw Material Sourcing
Hand-selected fruits from Kuršumlija's eco-certified orchards and partner farms across Serbia's prime growing regions.
2
Inspection & Sorting
Multi-stage quality inspection with automated and manual sorting to ensure only premium-grade fruit enters processing.
3
Processing & Packaging
Hot or cold processing under FSSC 22000 controls. IQF freezing, pasteurization, and aseptic packaging per product type.
4
Quality Assurance
Laboratory testing for microbiological safety, Brix levels, pH, and organoleptic properties before batch release.
5
Global Logistics
Temperature-controlled storage and transportation to customers across Europe, North America, and the Middle East.
